About Us

Who Are We?

NeuroSphere is an international youth-led organization dedicated to raising awareness about neurodegenerative diseases while mentoring and inspiring the next generation of neuroscience leaders.

Background

Founded in Winter 2024, NeuroSphere began when Valeria, a passionate high school senior, saw a lack of opportunities for students to explore neuroscience and felt driven to create a space that raises awareness of neurodegenerative diseases while inspiring future careers in the field.

What We Do

We publish weekly news carousels that shine a spotlight on neurodegenerative diseases, connect students with resources to pursue neuroscience in college, and offer chapter and leadership roles, research programs, internships, volunteer opportunities, and interactive virtual workshops.

General Membership

General membership is for individual students who want to join the wider NeuroSphere community. Middle school, high school, and undergraduate students are welcome to apply for access to programs, events, resources, and opportunities.
